Privacy Policy

Select & Chat · dev.jvald.selectandchat · Last updated 21 September 2026

Select & Chat does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It requests no Android permissions and makes no network requests of its own. Everything it remembers stays on your device.

What the app does

Select & Chat adds an item to Android's text-selection toolbar. When you select a phone number anywhere on your phone and tap that item, the app reads the text you selected, works out whether it is a phone number, and hands it to WhatsApp so the chat opens directly. The selected text is processed in memory on your device and is not sent anywhere by this app.

Data stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, the app saves the following in its own private storage:

  • Recent chats — the phone numbers you have opened through the app, so you can reuse them. This is optional and can be switched off under Save recent chats.
  • Quick messages — any message templates you create.
  • Settings — your theme, language and behaviour preferences.

This data is held in the app's private area, which no other app can read. It is never uploaded, never backed up to a server run by the developer, and is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

Data the app does not collect

  • No contacts. The app holds no contacts permission and does not read your address book.
  • No location, camera, microphone, files or any other permission-protected data — the app declares no permissions at all.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • No account, sign-in or identifier of any kind.

Deleting your data

Individual recent chats can be removed under Manage history, and clearing the whole history is available in the same place. Uninstalling the app removes everything it has stored.

WhatsApp

Select & Chat is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to WhatsApp or Meta. When the app opens a chat, WhatsApp takes over, and anything that happens from that point is covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy, not this one.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.
