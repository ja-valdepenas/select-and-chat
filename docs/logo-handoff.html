Hand-off, in pastel

Same idea: the line you were reading, and the chat it became. The bubble is round now, the tail is a soft nub instead of a spike, and the whole thing has dropped into a pastel register. Four palettes to choose the green by eye.

→

What changed in the drawing

The bubble was a 48×30 rounded rectangle with a straight-sided triangle stuck to it, which is what made it read as bubbly and off. It is now a true circle with a curved tail that grows out of the edge rather than sitting against it. The tail still aims at the highlighted stretch of text, so the sentence the icon tells is unchanged.

On borrowing their green

  • Colour is the low-risk half. Green runs across the whole messaging category and no one owns it. What the guidelines actually restrict is a mark confusable with theirs.
  • The silhouette is the exposed half. A saturated green tile holding a white round bubble with a tail is their icon's structure, and a circular bubble moves toward it. Two things hold this apart: the line of text underneath, which their icon has nothing of, and pastel, because their green is vivid #25D366 and a soft one reads as a different brand on sight.
  • Paper is the safe way to have it. It uses their green on the bubble and puts it on a near-white field, inverting the value structure. Mint is the closest to their arrangement: usable, and the one a reviewer would look at twice.
